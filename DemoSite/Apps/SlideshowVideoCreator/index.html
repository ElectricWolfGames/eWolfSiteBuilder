Slideshow Video Creator

Turn a folder of photos into a finished slideshow video, with titles, transitions and music, in a few clicks. Coming soon to the Microsoft Store.

Pick a folder of photos and Slideshow Video Creator turns it into an MP4 video, ready to play on a TV, share with family or upload to YouTube and Facebook.

Titles: An animated opening title card with the place, the date and who was there, plus an optional series title on every photo
Movement: Smooth transitions between photos (one style, a mix you choose, or random), and a gentle pan across portrait photos, which you can position yourself
Your music: Background music from your own music folder, with volume and fades
Choosing photos: Untick the ones you don't want, rotate any photo, and put them in name order, date-taken order or your own order
Preview: Watch a short preview of the title card and first photos in seconds, before making the whole video
Video settings: 720p, 1080p or 4K, at 24 to 60 frames per second
Big folders: Hundreds of photos, including OneDrive folders
Private: Everything happens on your PC. Nothing is uploaded, and the app collects no personal data (privacy policy)


Slideshow Video Creator is finished and on its way to the Microsoft Store, for Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Requirements


Windows: Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit
iPhone photos (HEIC): Need Microsoft's HEVC Video Extensions (a small paid add-on from the Microsoft Store) and the free HEIF Image Extension. JPEG, PNG and most other photos need nothing extra
Windows "N" editions: Need Microsoft's free Media Feature Pack before videos can be made. The app checks and explains
