Number Trail

One line, one to ten, one shot at it. Coming soon to Google Play.

Coming soon to Google Play
A one line puzzle game for Android. Numbered coins drift across the board, and the job is to trace them in order without lifting your finger.
Touch the wrong coin and the whole trail breaks, and you start the chain again with the clock still running.

Beat the timer and every spare second is added to your score. Run it out and it costs you a life.

Three ways to play: Level runs the numbers in order, Challenging leaves gaps in the sequence, and Daily gives every player the same ten boards
A new set every day: Ten daily levels built from the date, identical for everyone, with the last seven days of results kept on the menu — green for passed, red for failed, grey for not played
Find it, do not count to it: Challenging widens the gaps as you climb, so you are hunting the next number rather than counting up to it
Decoys in the way: Blank, unnumbered coins start appearing early on. They are not part of the chain, so the line has to go around them
It keeps getting harder: Five coins become ten, the clock tightens every couple of levels, and later on the coins stop holding still and drift while you trace
Three lives: Running the clock out costs one, and the run ends when the last one goes
Beat the clock for a bonus: Finish early and the time left on the timer is converted straight into points
See where the points went: Every finished level is itemised — coins, level bonus, time bonus, and what a broken trail cost you
Fair by construction: Boards are generated so the correct path never passes within touch range of a coin that would break it, and every level is stress tested after any change to the rules
Built for one hand: Portrait, one finger, no account and no sign-up. Most runs take a few minutes


Number Trail is finished and in testing. It will be free to download and play on any Android phone or tablet.
