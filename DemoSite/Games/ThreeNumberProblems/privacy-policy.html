Privacy Policy

Three Number Problem · Effective 2 August 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Three Number Problem ("the App", package com.threenumberproblem.game) handles information. The App is published by ElectricWolf ("we", "us").

Information we collect directly

None. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no registration. We do not ask for, collect, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

The App saves a small amount of gameplay data locally on your device so your progress and preferences persist between sessions:

  • Your current streak and best streak
  • Your music and sound effect on/off preferences

This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us, and is not accessible to us. It is deleted automatically when you uninstall the App.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements, and this is the only way information about you is collected. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), a mediation service operated by Unity Technologies, together with the ad networks it mediates.

To serve, cap, and measure ads, these third-party services may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID)
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and network type
  • Your IP address, and coarse location derived from it (such as country)
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. It is handled by Unity and its ad partners under their own privacy policies. Please refer to Unity's Privacy Policy for details on what they collect and how it is used.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset your advertising ID or delete it entirely to opt out of personalised advertising.
  • Delete all local data. Uninstalling the App removes everything it has stored on your device.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will address it.

Your rights (EEA, UK, and California)

Because we do not collect or hold personal information ourselves, we have no user data to access, correct, or delete on request. Rights relating to advertising data collected by Unity and its ad partners — including access, deletion, and objecting to personalised advertising — should be exercised through Unity using the contact routes in their privacy policy, or by resetting your advertising ID as described above.

Data retention

We retain no personal data, as we collect none. Locally stored gameplay data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its storage. Retention of advertising data is governed by Unity and its ad partners.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when we do. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
